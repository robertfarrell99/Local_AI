## Part A: Student records retention schedule (extract)

| Record type | How long it is kept | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core student record (name, dates of study, award, classification) | Permanently | Kept |
| Full transcript of marks | Permanently | Kept |
| Application and admissions papers for students who enrolled | 6 years after the student leaves | Destroyed |
| Applications that did not lead to enrolment | 1 year after the end of the admissions cycle | Destroyed |
| Disciplinary and complaints files | 6 years after the case closes | Destroyed |
| Visa sponsorship records | 1 year after sponsorship ends, or until the next compliance audit, whichever is later | Destroyed |
| Financial records of fees | 7 years after the end of the financial year | Destroyed |

Records that must be kept permanently may be held in an archive rather than the live system, provided they can be searched and a verified copy can be produced within 5 working days of a request.

Any change to where permanent records are held must be agreed with the University Records Manager before it happens.

## Part C: Training plan

Training is online, in three modules, taking about 40 minutes in total. About 600 staff need to complete it. The target is 85% completion by Friday 29 January 2027 and 100% before go-live.

The training budget is £380,000. Of this, £95,000 is for the online modules, £210,000 is for backfill so staff can be released, and £75,000 is for floor-walkers in the first four weeks after go-live.
