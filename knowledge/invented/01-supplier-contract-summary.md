# Meridian Programme — Supplier contract summary (Larchfield Software)

INVENTED TEST DOCUMENT — fictional organisations. Not a real contract.

Prepared by: Programme Office, University of Wexcombe
Version 2.1, 14 September 2026

## 1. Parties and term

The University of Wexcombe ("the University") has a contract with Larchfield Software Ltd ("the Supplier") for the supply and implementation of the Larchfield Campus student records system. The contract was signed on 3 March 2025 and runs for an initial term of seven years from the date of first go-live, with an option to extend by up to three further years.

## 2. Implementation charges and milestones

The fixed implementation charge is £1,150,000, paid against six milestones. A milestone is achieved only when the University has confirmed in writing that the deliverable has passed acceptance testing.

| Milestone | Deliverable | Payment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Contract signature and mobilisation | £115,000 |
| 2 | Design sign-off | £230,000 |
| 3 | Core configuration delivered | £285,000 |
| 4 | Release 7.3, including the visa sponsorship module | £120,000 |
| 5 | Core records and enrolment go-live | £250,000 |
| 6 | End of early life support (90 days after go-live) | £150,000 |

Milestones 1 to 3 have been paid, a total of £630,000.

## 3. Late delivery and service credits

If the Supplier delivers a milestone later than the date in the agreed plan, the University may claim a service credit of 2% of that milestone's payment for each full week of delay. Service credits for any one milestone are capped at 10% of the milestone's payment.

Service credits are deducted from the milestone payment. They are not paid in cash.

To claim service credits the University must give written notice to the Supplier within 20 working days of the missed date. The notice must come from the University's named contract manager. A claim made after 20 working days is not valid.

## 4. Termination for delay

If any milestone is more than 16 weeks late, the University may terminate the contract with 30 days' written notice. On termination for delay the Supplier refunds 50% of the implementation charges paid to date.

## 5. Annual charges after go-live

From first go-live the University pays an annual licence and support charge of £310,000, rising each year by the lower of 4% or the Consumer Prices Index.

## 6. Optional modules

Optional modules can be added by a contract change note. Prices quoted by the Supplier are valid for 60 days from the date of the quote. The degree apprenticeships module is an optional module and is not included in the implementation charge.

## 7. Named contacts

The University's named contract manager is the Programme Director. The Supplier's named contact is its Delivery Lead. Escalation is to the University's Chief Operating Officer and the Supplier's Chief Executive.
